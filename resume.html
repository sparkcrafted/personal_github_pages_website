Warren A. Jones II is a strategic operations leader with nearly two decades of experience in nonprofit and mission-driven organizations. His work focuses on executive operations, board governance, organizational systems, business intelligence, and applied analytics.

His career has centered on helping organizations translate strategy into usable systems: governance workflows, executive dashboards, procurement processes, cross-functional reporting, data infrastructure, and decision-support tools. Warren’s résumé reflects a progression from administrative and program operations into chief of staff leadership, independent consulting, and national board governance.

Profile

Strategic operations, governance, and applied analytics leader with experience supporting executive teams, boards of directors, enterprise operations, and cross-functional initiatives in complex nonprofit environments. Skilled at building systems that improve visibility, decision-making, accountability, and organizational follow-through.

Warren’s work sits at the intersection of operations management, governance, business intelligence, and data analytics. He is especially interested in how mission-driven organizations use information systems, dashboards, workflows, and applied data science to solve business problems they often lack the capacity or infrastructure to address.
