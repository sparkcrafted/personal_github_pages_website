Warren A. Jones II is a strategic operations, governance, and analytics leader with two decades of experience in mission-driven organizations. His work has included executive leadership support, board governance, procurement and contracts oversight, enterprise systems modernization, business intelligence, and decision-support infrastructure.

His professional and applied research interests center on how nonprofit and mission-driven organizations use data infrastructure, information systems, analytics, and knowledge management to strengthen operations, improve resource allocation, and sustain organizational impact.

Professional profile

Strategic operations, governance, and analytics leader with two decades of experience in mission-driven organizations, including executive leadership, team supervision, procurement and contracts oversight, enterprise systems modernization, board governance, and business intelligence. Applies data infrastructure, information systems, and decision-support tools to improve organizational effectiveness, resource allocation, and operational capacity.

Warren’s work sits at the intersection of nonprofit operations, governance, business intelligence, knowledge management, and applied analytics. His research interests include nonprofit sustainability, governance systems, applied analytics, and data infrastructure for mission-driven organizations.
